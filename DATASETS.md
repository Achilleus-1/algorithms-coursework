# Dataset provenance

The nearest-store query coordinates are maintenance examples of approximate
public city centers (New York, Los Angeles, Chicago, Houston, Phoenix, Seattle,
Miami and Boston), rounded to two decimals. They are not home locations or
original assignment query points. They may be reused under the maintenance license.

The existing store/business datasets and convex-hull/word-segmentation inputs
were supplied with the coursework import. Their original upstream source and
redistribution terms have not been verified; do not treat publication here as
a new data license. Store contact information describes businesses. Review
data provenance before copying these inputs into another project.
